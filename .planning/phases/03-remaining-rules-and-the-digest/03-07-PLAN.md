<objective>
Make `doctor` report the Phase 3 settings an operator must get right: when the digest goes out and whether the score rules are switched on.

Purpose: the score rules ship off (D-08), so a silent "no low scores" can mean "no problem" or "no threshold set"; the digest depends on a window that must be at least as long as the timer tick, or the "always sent" heartbeat (DIG-02) can silently miss its week. Both are config facts `doctor` can read, with no new network call.
Output: two verdict functions, two lines in the doctor report, tests.
</objective>
