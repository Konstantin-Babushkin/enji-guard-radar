## Notes

- Why a bumped version and not optional fields in schema 2: an older radar must refuse a file it cannot fully understand, exactly as it refuses 3 today ("this radar reads 1 and 2"). Rolling back the radar after Phase 3 ran therefore halts it loudly instead of silently dropping the digest and reminder clocks.
- `migrate_state_v1` is left alone: it keeps returning schema_version 2, and `deserialize_state` chains it into `migrate_state_v2`.
- `ConditionRecord` is not changed (its field-set test in tests/test_state_v2.py stays as it is): reminders keep their own clock in `ReminderClock`, and a series' effective clock is the later of the High condition's `alerted_at` and its `last_reminded_at` (plan 03-04 and 03-10).
