## Notes

- Why a clock never needs pruning: the effective clock of a series is the later of its High condition's `alerted_at` and its `ReminderClock`. A series that closes and reopens gets a new alert, whose `alerted_at` is later than any old reminder; a series without `alerted_at` has no clock at all. A leftover clock for a closed series is therefore harmless and bounded by the number of series that ever got a reminder.
- Why a series that was only baselined is not reminded: D-11 counts the interval "from the last alert or reminder sent for that series", and SC5 speaks of re-sending; a series the group never heard about as an alert is in every weekly digest instead (D-04). A rise re-arms it into a real alert and starts its clock.
- A partially delivered multi-message reminder (two chunks, the second fails) marks only the series of the acknowledged chunk; the other chunk's series are simply due again on the next run.
