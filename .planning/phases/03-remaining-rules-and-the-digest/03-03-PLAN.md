## Notes

- Decision table, with m = planned_moment(now) and elapsed = now - m: `handled_for == m` is NotDue; elapsed below grace is Due when the poll is ok and Deferred when it is not; elapsed at or above grace is Skipped when `handled_for` is an earlier moment, and NotDue when `handled_for` is None (a fresh install) or later than m (clock moved back).
- grace is at most 168 hours (plan 03-02), so only one planned moment is ever open at a time.
- The Skipped decision carries the moment so the pipeline can record it once and log "a week was skipped" once (D-02 and the log line in Claude's Discretion).
