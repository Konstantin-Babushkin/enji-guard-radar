## Notes

- A reminder item reads: "- <b>{project}</b>: {N High finding(s)} in {audit type} ({repo}). Seen by the radar since {date} ({N days})." The header line is "<b>Reminder: High findings are still open</b>", with " (k/n)" appended inside the bold when the text is split. "Seen by the radar since" keeps the Phase 2 D-02 wording so older history is never implied.
- Marking a reminder as sent (writing `ReminderClock`) is plan 03-10's job, after Telegram acknowledges; this plan only decides and renders.
- Series keys: a High alert's AlertKey (project_id, repo_id, action_key, level "high") maps to the series key (project_id, repo_id, action_key).
