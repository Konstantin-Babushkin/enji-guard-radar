## Notes

- Why scores are read regardless of a threshold: the report is read for the High rules anyway under the default `audit_types: null`; keeping the score costs nothing then, and with a narrowed `audit_types` it costs one read per repository and score audit type per new run (two per repository per week at the observed cadence). It also means the first `probe`-era data is already in state when the operator sets a threshold (D-08), with no one-poll delay.
- `RunReport.low_scores` and `tooling_gaps` are what the digest step of plan 03-11 consumes; they are also the way tests observe the rules.
- A repository the poll could not read is a failed project (unknown): its records stay. A repository removed from a complete listing loses its records through `evaluate_scores` and `evaluate_tooling`, like its High history does.
- D-07 "resolved" counts keys that `reconcile` resolved. That includes the dropped series of a removed repository; the digest shows it as a closed High series, which is accurate enough for a count.
