<objective>
Make the Guard parsing strict, complete and safe: Markdown audit reports parse page by page into typed reports, every timestamp and enum is parsed defensively, and any failed, empty or partial fetch becomes "unknown" instead of looking like "no runs" or "no findings" (SRC-03, SRC-04). The fixture source becomes a hardened read-only replay (SRC-06).

Purpose: rules in this and later phases are only as trustworthy as the snapshot. A guessed or partial snapshot creates false alarms or silences real ones (docs/guard-data-notes.md; PITFALLS 1, 2 and 9).
Output: `guard_report.py`, report reading wired into `fetch_project`, a read-only tool allowlist, and table tests for timestamps, enums, reports, paging and failure isolation.
</objective>
