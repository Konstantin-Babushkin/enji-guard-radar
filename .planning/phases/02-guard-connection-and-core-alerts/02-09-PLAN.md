## Design notes (Claude's discretion per CONTEXT.md)

- Allowlist, not denylist: per tool only known structural fields are written; any other field is dropped and counted in the summary, so new Guard fields become visible without leaking.
- Multi-page reports are written as one page (has_more false) for the first call with the task_id; their cursor calls are not recorded. List cursors become `<opaque-cursor-N>` (numbered, because the manifest forbids two recordings of the same tool and arguments).
- A failed call is recorded as an `error` entry with the fixed text "recorded failure (<cause>)".
- Severity words are kept as they are when they are one word of letters (at most 20), because observing a word above High (research Pitfall 9) is the point of probing. ASSUMPTION A13 (no severity above High) and A12 (report layouts) stay unverified until the first real probe; 02-RESEARCH.md Open Question 7 is RESOLVED as "the probe summary lists them and any parser change goes through a gap-closure plan".
