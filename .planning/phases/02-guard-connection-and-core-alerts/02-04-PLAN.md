<objective>
Switch the High-count rules on inside the real run: for every evaluated project, fold the newest completed run of each (repo, audit type) into the stored history (plan 02-01), turn open series into alerts (plan 02-02), store the histories in State before reconcile, and let the existing ledger baseline, send, re-arm and resolve them. Reports of runs already in history are not read again, which keeps the steady-state poll cost to the list calls plus genuinely new reports.

Purpose: this is where RULE-02, RULE-03, RULE-09, STATE-07 and ALERT-01 become observable to the group (Phase 2 success criteria 2 and 3), on fixtures and fakes, before any live Guard exists.
Output: `evaluate_high` and `skip_report_for` in `high_rules.py`, pipeline wiring, the `skip_report` predicate in `guard.py`, Guard fake builders, the tracer and edge table, and updated baseline counts in the Phase 1 end-to-end tests.
</objective>
